**Experimental Plan: A. pulchra gene expression microscope photographs**

> **Archive status:** this appears to describe a July photo-only set, not
> the May-June main experiment. Treat it as the plan for representative imagery
> unless the source data show these photos were also used for analysis.

**Objective:** Take a daily time series of wounded *A. pulchra* under heat stress using the same core procedure as the gene-expression corals. The purpose is to produce an illustrative image series that matches the wound-healing traits scored in the larger (n = 192) gene-expression x wound experiment. This image series is not a substitute for the full analyzed morphology dataset.

**Species:** A. pulchra (n=16)

| Factor | Levels |
| ----- | ----- |
| Temperature | 2 (Ambient (28C), Heated (31C)) |
| Treatment | 1 (Wounded only; no unwounded control in this photo-only set) |
| Timepoints | 22 (sequential days 0-15) |
| Thickets | 2 (A and C; thicket D not included, rationale to verify) |

**Tank Design:**

* 4 tanks: 2 ambient tanks; 2 heated tanks; 2 thickets (A and C) represented in each tank
* Within each tank:
  * 2 fragments from each thicket

**Microscope settings:**

* Each coral is photographed at 1.25x and 3.2x zoom. Rationale for these magnifications should be stated before using this as a methods protocol.
* Photo naming scheme   
  * Key: `species_treatment_coral-id_day_zoom`
  * Ex: `apulchra_ambient_12_D3_3.2x`
* Aperture and dissection light source were adjusted for consistency between images. In most sessions, aperture was set near the low end on the microscope and light-source intensity was 85-95%.
* Camera exposure was kept consistent at 169ms, 0 gain  
* Entire microscope mount was raised 2.5cm to accommodate coral height  
* All photos were saved as vsi and converted to 8-bit color images and exported as .tif

**Schedule:**

June 17: Collect 16 *A. pulchra* (verify whether n = 8 per thicket or 8 total from thickets A and C), cut to ~6 cm tall, and glue to square tiles. Treat every fragment for 1 h with "Worm Exit" (20 L water / 4 drops Worm Exit) and blast off any worms with a transfer pipet. Place in mesocosm at ambient temperature (~28 C). See metadata for thicket coordinates.

June 18-27: Allow corals to acclimate to mesocosm light and water-quality conditions.

June 27: Split corals so that there are 4 corals in each tank, 2 from each thicket. Begin heat ramp for 2 tanks (n=8 total, n=4 per thicket).

June 30: Hold heat-ramp corals at 31 C. 28 C corals continue at ambient temperature.

July 5: Wound corals by cutting ~1 cm off the tip with a band saw. This protocol is intended to match the gene-expression study. Photograph corals top-down on the microscope at 1.25x and 3.2x zoom daily in the same orientation. State how orientation was fixed day to day before using this as a formal protocol.

July 5-July 20: Photograph corals (n = 16) daily. Use the [physio characterization log](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1_6CCmYHGpocI7q9XDzaJHya5NaR-SqArgd24aOSpuoM/edit?usp=sharing) to determine which yes/no morphology trait each picture represents. Record yes/no for each of the following traits:

1. Tissue over wound  
2. Hole in center  
3. Polyp in hole  
4. Wound smoothed  
5. Pigment over wound  
6. Tip exists / new apical tip present
7. Tip extension  
8. New corallites on tip

The goal is to produce a single illustrative series of images for heated and ambient healing corals, and not all days may be represented in this image series. This image series may complement the initial [characterization of *A. pulchra* wound healing](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1wfShEwuEjSP6DPPs6qeFxOPWtqXPlF5Jf_pgiHlPLUo/edit?usp=sharing).
